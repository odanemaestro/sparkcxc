# SPARK Paper 2 Simulator

Paper 2 now runs fully inside the SPARK practice area.

## Exam structure

- 10 compulsory structured questions
- 2 hours 40 minutes
- 100 marks
- Section I: Questions 1 to 7, 64 marks
- Section II: Questions 8 to 10, 36 marks
- Historical Paper 2 topic order retained
- Graph-drawing and geometric-construction tasks excluded

## Question bank

- 60 original SPARK structured questions
- 6 question variants for each historical Paper 2 position
- One question is selected for each position in every generated paper
- Duplicate question IDs are blocked inside a paper
- Previously unseen questions are preferred across later attempts until a position's pool has been exhausted

## In-platform response and grading

- Every structured question is divided into marked answer parts
- Students enter final answers directly into individual mathematical answer fields
- Numeric, fraction, coordinate, vector, matrix and supported algebraic responses are graded automatically
- Configured rounding tolerances are applied where questions request decimal-place accuracy
- Partial credit is awarded by subpart rather than requiring a tutor to mark the complete paper
- Results are shown immediately after submission with correct answers and worked solutions

## UX

- 2:40:00 live countdown
- Autosave and resume on the same browser
- Question navigator for Sections I and II
- Automatic completion state based on filled answer fields
- Flag-for-review state
- In-platform formula sheet
- Mathematical symbol toolbar
- Immediate post-exam score and worked review
- Restyled Back, Formula Sheet, Previous, Next and Submit controls with explicit hover, focus and mobile states

## Academic source gates

1. Preserve exactly the source's numbered question and the four printed choices. Two-column extraction interleaves questions and options, so raw page text must never be published directly as a question bank.
2. Verify any original graphs, charts, tables and figures against the source page before enabling scoring. Keep image-dependent questions excluded until figures are faithfully reproduced.
3. Inspect the 2018 gap before describing this paper as a complete 60-item simulation. Do not invent Questions 53–57.
4. Separate source transcription accuracy from answer-key verification. Existing SPARK-authored answer keys are **not** official CXC answer keys.
5. Only register a complete source paper when all 60 original numbered stems and four choices are verified. Preserve unavailable papers as reference-only in the meantime.
6. Validate mobile/tablet/desktop question rendering and exam timer/review behaviour for every newly integrated sitting.

# SPARK copy style

Use clear Caribbean examination English.

- Do not use em dashes.
- Use short, direct sentences.
- For examination instructions, use formal verbs such as Calculate, Determine, State, Find, Show and Give.
- Use wording such as "Answer ALL questions" and "giving your answer correct to ..." where appropriate.
- Avoid promotional or conversational wording inside examination screens.
- Keep mathematical notation separate from explanatory prose where possible.
- Do not change a mathematical minus sign into a hyphen when the minus sign is part of an expression.

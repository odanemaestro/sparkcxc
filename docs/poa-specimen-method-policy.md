## Revenue-unit erratum v1

Erratum identifier: `poa-specimen-2017-5c-revenue-units-v1`, part 5(c), SPARK-authored arithmetic correction.

The given selling price is $3,000 per unit. Quarterly sales of 3,000/4,000/7,000/8,000 units produce revenues of $9,000,000/$12,000,000/$21,000,000/$24,000,000, totaling $66,000,000. The printed key's 9,000/12,000/21,000/24,000/66,000 row is smaller by 1,000 and lacks a thousands heading.

Accept either the correct dollar figures or the equivalent smaller figures with an **explicit revenue-row $000/thousands-of-dollars declaration**. The original unit price and unit quantities remain unchanged. A candidate may explicitly replace the form's revenue-dollar unit with a $000 heading in working, so the legacy form's printed label alone does not invalidate a clear correction.

Do not accept smaller figures claimed as dollars, unlabeled smaller values, a changed unit price of $3, or contradictory dollar/thousands declarations. A wrong revenue scale loses the affected revenue points and retains correct independent setup/unit points. Correct answers to other subparts retain their marks. The original PDF remains unchanged and the policy source identity retains its hash.

## Method and own figures

An answer point can be earned independently of a method point where listed. A bare $8 per-unit answer earns its answer point, but cannot prove division. A correct method with a fresh arithmetic error retains only the applicable method point. An arbitrary matching number in an unrelated assertion does not earn an accounting mark.

Follow-through is explicitly enabled for net profit from a claimed gross profit; gross-profit percentage from a claimed gross profit; working capital from claimed current assets; net assets from claimed working capital; closing capital from claimed net profit; current ratio from claimed current assets; petty cash balance/reimbursement from the candidate's claimed earlier payments/balance; manufacturing materials-used/prime-cost/cost-before-WIP/production-cost chains; and unit cost from claimed production cost.

Every follow-through award requires a single unambiguous prior claim, the correct demonstrated later operation and a consistent result within the point's rounding tolerance. Wrong signs, reversed ratios, inconsistent calculations and conflicting candidate claims do not qualify. A student does not have to use the words “own figure.” No identity formula validates an arbitrary self-declared total. Upstream marks are evaluated separately, avoiding an additional penalty merely because the same upstream error reappears in a later correct method.

## Evidence, alternatives and review

The actual specimen response shape is preserved: question-level `working`, free-text `parts` for text sections and generated `cells` for printed tables. Debit/credit meaning comes from account sides/columns, never solely from a matching amount. Equivalent account labels, reordered complete transactions, decimal/percentage method alternatives and the three balanced share-issue entries are supported. A duplicated alias consumes one list concept, not two answer slots.

The deterministic recognizers are bounded. Supported assertions require coherent account/method context and reject contradictions. Unknown semantic alternatives, unreadable references and ambiguous competing claims stay available for review; they are not silently published as a final zero. In particular, the source's “other appropriate answers” permission is not reduced to a claim that an unseen correct answer is wrong. The supported concept pool can be extended in a new calibrated policy version.

`src/principlesOfAccounts/marking/poaSpecimenMethodPolicy.js` contains all 100 typed points and their standalone correct, alternative and incorrect proof examples. `src/principlesOfAccounts/calibration/poaSpecimenMethodFixtures.js` contains independent full-question scripts, partial scripts, equivalent journals, exact numeric counterexamples, follow-through cases, duplicate lists, contradictions and the two accepted versus rejected revenue-unit forms. The fixtures import neither the policy nor the evaluator and their expected marks are authored decisions. Passing them is calibration evidence; the private V2 registry and server publication gates still decide whether a particular policy/bundle release is active.

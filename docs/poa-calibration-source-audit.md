## Concrete calibration cases and expected decisions

Fixture responses must be handwritten independently of evaluator output. Never alter an expected mark merely to match the grader. Safe initial cases include complete source-consistent answers, clearly irrelevant answers, exact semantic alternatives and deliberate contradictions. List partials can be calibrated under an explicit, versioned SPARK distinct-answer policy. Arithmetic partials and own-figure marks must remain unresolved until an actual allocation policy is specified; preserving their response and diagnostics is preferable to inventing a score.

`src/principlesOfAccounts/calibration/poaSpecimenSourceCases.js` contains 45 independently written candidate scripts covering all 22 parts: 22 complete reference responses and 23 edge cases. Thirteen expected marks deliberately remain `null`; the others identify their authority explicitly, including proposed SPARK list policy rather than official per-point allocations. All 45 were bundled and passed through the current adapter's source-bound input validation: their field IDs are valid, case IDs are unique and numeric expectations do not exceed the printed caps. This is an input/reference check, **not** a passing grader calibration run. The candidates are outside the automatic V2 fixture runner and no registration, approval or release was performed.

## Evaluator findings at audit start

1. `evaluatePoaSpecimenSourceV2` intentionally returns `suggestedMarks:0, resolved:false` for every nonblank criterion. This is a safe placeholder, not completed calibration. Matching figures are diagnostics only. It cannot yet distinguish a complete cash book from a single matching balance, or a reversed journal from the correct double entry.
2. The adapter currently validates the legacy generated `answerSections` shape. The actual specimen still uses those sections, because its printed blocks have no `answerSpace` flags. If the specimen is later converted to native editable source forms, its cell bindings and calibration fixtures must change together; silently reading obsolete cell IDs would discard valid student evidence.
3. Method evidence is accepted through the worksheet's working/details cells and the question-level `working` string. Any new evaluator must reconcile contradictory answer cells with free text rather than award marks when a correct phrase appears anywhere. Recognition of exact known full scripts can be useful as a bounded first policy, but unsupported variants must remain unresolved.
4. The source identity check is strong: it binds all 22 criteria, caps, question/part metadata, checkpoint bindings and source SHA, and tolerates JSONB object-key order. Retain this before any scoring.
5. `working` currently has only a string type check, while individual `parts` and `cells` strings are capped at 200,000 characters. Apply the same bound to `working` if this adapter becomes a runtime evaluator, and keep whole-request server limits.
6. A wholly blank script is resolved as zero by the shared server engine. That does not authorize publishing nonblank source-conflicted work or releasing the paper without its release evidence.

## Release evidence required

The existing V2 process requires source-bound full, partial, incorrect, alternative, contradiction and relevant own-figure cases; matching rubric/source fingerprints; the same server examiner bundle used in tests and worker; clean-commit provenance; and paper-level end-to-end evidence. The private registry rejects incomplete or failing evidence and verifies queue, lease, retry, owner-only result access and atomic score publication. The Accounts course and its rubrics must remain draft until their own evidence meets those gates.

For this particular specimen, the 7-mark 5(c) conflict independently prevents an honest complete score out of 100. A selected known-answer success or 93 marks of recognizable evidence cannot make that missing decision disappear. A later erratum policy must state the accepted money units and marking treatment, be versioned with the rubric, include both unscaled and explicitly scaled student answers, and be tested against the unchanged source.

Historical Paper 2 worked solutions are independently calculated study guidance. They do not become official marking schemes by being complete. Historical papers need their own authored allocations and calibration evidence before authoritative V2 marks can be published.

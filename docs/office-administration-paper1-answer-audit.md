# Independent review of original Office Administration Paper 1 answers

Reviewed 2026-10-26: `OA-ORIGINAL-P1-01-q01` through `q60` and `OA-ORIGINAL-P1-02-q01` through `q60` (120 questions). The reviewer read every stem, all four options and every worked explanation, resolved the answer, and then compared it with the authored key. The report concerns SPARK original practice items; it does not certify a historical CXC answer key.

**Result: 120/120 keys and worked explanations accepted; no answer correction required.** Every question has one defensible best answer among its offered options. The numerical items were recalculated independently. The union of objective tags covers the 80 objectives in the supplied current syllabus. This is a content review, not empirical item calibration or an assurance that these items reproduce the difficulty of a CXC examination.

The current sets provide introductory practice. Many distractors describe obviously irrelevant or improper behaviour. A future difficulty review could replace those distractors with plausible mistakes; that is not a scoring defect in the current questions.

The entries below identify the exact question by its paper ID and number. For example, paper 01 question 41 is `OA-ORIGINAL-P1-01-q41`.

About the Academy

SPARK Sports Academy provides structured after-school training in swimming, football, netball and athletics for young athletes.

Programmes and Fees

Swimming: $9,000 monthly. Football: $7,500 monthly. Netball: $7,000 monthly. Athletics: $8,000 monthly.

Training Schedule

Training sessions take place on selected weekdays and Saturdays. Families should confirm the current training days before registration.

Registration

Complete the athlete registration form and submit the required guardian and medical information.

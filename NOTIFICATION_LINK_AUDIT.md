# Notification link audit

The Notification Centre now routes each supported notification to its intended destination.

- Student booking notifications open My bookings and focus the booking.
- Tutor booking notifications open My sessions and focus the booking.
- Student Paper 1 and Paper 2 notifications open Progress and focus the matching result.
- Parent Paper 1 and Paper 2 notifications select the correct child and focus the matching exam result.
- Parent booking notifications select the correct child and focus the related tutor session.
- Family requests open the student's family approval card.
- Family connection updates open the parent's family area.
- Tutor application updates open the tutor application page.

No additional Supabase migration is required for this patch.
